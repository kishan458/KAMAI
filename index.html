Under the hood

Three original algorithms. No fabricated data.

Kamai isn't a wrapper around a spreadsheet. Every screen is backed by logic built specifically for how quick-commerce delivery actually works.

01 / Scarcity Scoring

Where is demand outrunning supply?

Ranks every darkstore by how far its active rider count sits below a baseline coverage level.

score = max(0, 8 − activeRiders) HOT ≥ 5 · WARM ≥ 2 · else BALANCED
02 / Controlled Release

Solves the "everyone rushes" problem.

Uses KNN (K-Nearest Neighbors) to identify the most relevant riders so Only the nearest N riders needed to fill the gap get notified — never the whole city at once.

if score ≥ 3: notify closest N riders (N = score, radius 5km)
03 / Earnings Ranking

Which platform is really worth your hours?

Compares your own logged effective rate across platforms and surfaces what switching would be worth.

rate(P) = netAfterDeductions / hours loyaltyPenalty = (bestRate − yourRate) × hrs
